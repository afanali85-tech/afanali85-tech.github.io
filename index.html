01 / ABOUT

Research at the intersection of AI and the physical world.

AFAN ALI (Senior Member, IEEE) received the B.Sc. degree in telecommunications engineering from the National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES), Islamabad, Pakistan, in 2008, the M.Sc. degree in engineering from Australian National University (ANU), Canberra, Australia, in 2011, and the Ph.D. degree in information and communication engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU), Xi’an, China, in 2018.

From 2009 to 2024, he served at the National Engineering and Scientific Commission (NESCOM), Islamabad, Pakistan, where he progressed through successive research leadership roles. From 2024 to 2025, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Communications, Signal Processing, and Networking Center (CoSiNC) at Istanbul Medipol University, Istanbul, Türkiye. He is currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Communication Systems and Sensing (IRC-CSS), King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM), Saudi Arabia.

He received the Best Paper Award at IEEE IMCEC 2016 (Xi’an, China) and the China Outstanding International Student Award (Shaanxi Province, 2018). His research lies at the intersection of wireless communications, active and predictive digital twins, and Generative and Agentic AI for 6G networks.

02 / RESEARCH

Research interests & directions

From intelligent network representations to timely decisions in dynamic physical environments.

01

Digital Twins

Cognitive and predictive digital twins for wireless systems, real-time network representations, and self-aware channel estimation.

02

Physical AI

Closed-loop sensing, reasoning, and action that accounts for mobility, physical dynamics, and decision latency.

03

Agentic AI

Autonomous, decision-centric agents for adaptive network control, predictive handover, and intelligent sensing.

04

Wireless Communications

Beyond-5G/6G, integrated sensing and communications, NTN, channel estimation, and physical-layer intelligence.

05

Generative AI

Generative and adversarial learning for proactive interference management, secure communications, and future-state prediction.

06

Trustworthy Edge Intelligence

Resource-aware learning, TinyML, robust signal classification, and reliable AI in constrained systems.

CURRENT RESEARCH PROGRAM

Design of Digital Twins for Wireless Systems

Contributing to KFUPM-sponsored research (SAR 600,000), alongside work on decision-centric predictive sensing, cognitive digital twins, and autonomous 6G networks.
